# Rendering coverage: what the game draws against what our ray tracing handles (2026-10-27)

This is a data-driven matrix of BG3's rendering, per pass and shader family, against the
layer's RT effects. Its purpose is to rank the remaining gaps by on-screen impact. It is
not a re-analysis of the game files: it uses the captures, the QA results, the asset DB
and the layer's own classification code.

**Regenerate the matrix after new captures:** `python -m scripts.qa.coverage --update-doc`
(about 30 s). It scans `captures/2026*`, every worktree's `captures/2026*` and
`captures/qa/*/S*`, and rewrites the block between the markers below. The ranked gaps and
the asset-DB tables are written by hand.

## How it is measured (`scripts/qa/coverage.py`)

- **Passes** come from each capture's `frame.json`. They are classified by attachment
  formats (FRAME_MAP), then by their draws' fragment shaders, named through the asset DB
  (`data/assetdb`):
  - the G-buffer has 5 targets and the decal pass 4;
  - a B10G11R11 + D24S8 pass is the effects target when it holds `*Emissive` (EMI)
    variants or the sky. It is the compose pass for `5c3fdabc`, the SSS blur for
    `6f535075`/`d1669194`, and otherwise a forward pass.
- **Families** inside the effects and forward passes come from the template name and the
  DB flags: water, lava/emissive, glass/refractive, VFX/particles, other transparent, or
  unknown FS (not in the DB).
- **Screen share** is measured on the dumps of up to 4 captures per scene (`--measure`):
  - the pixels a pass changed against the previous dump of the same image (the forward
    passes against the compose output; never against the SSS scratch use of that image);
  - a fresh effects target: its non-zero pixels;
  - the G-buffer's material classes from RT2.a & 7, at the preview resolution (1280 wide).
  - "G-buffer: empty" is depth = 0 in the G-buffer: sky, or surfaces drawn only by the
    forward pass (the S1 sea).
  - A pass's share goes to a family only when that family has ≥ 90% of the pass's draws.
    Otherwise it is in the pass-kind table only.
- **Scenes** come from `run.json`, the QA folder, `captures/queue/results.jsonl` with the
  job files, or `captures/scene-sweep.json`. Captures without a known scene are "?".
- **Handling columns** come from the `HANDLING` table in the script, which mirrors
  TRACK_D_RT.md 9.10 (casters/receivers), 10 (D-RF), 12 (sun replace) and 13 (D-EM). Update
  it when an effect changes. Columns: **caster** in the TLAS; receives the RT **sun**, the
  RT **local** shadows (D-LS), RT **GI**/AO, **refl**ections (D-RF); its emission captured by
  **D-EM** ("branch": `worktree-agent-a2212c228ec492214`, not in main).

**Caveats:**
- S1's save `QuickSave_3784` now opens on the docks: 5 of its 6 measured captures show the
  sea, and the scene sweep's 2026-10-26 capture shows the foundry interior. The
  foundry-only captures without a scene are under "?".
- Shares depend on the view. The median over the measured captures is shown, and the
  maximum when it is far higher (S6's lava covers 26.7% of the effects target in the D-EM capture `agent-a2212c228ec492214/captures/20261027-072936`, and 1.0% in the median of the 4 views measured by default).
- The forward share is per pass: a family gets it only when it dominates the pass. Per-draw
  pixel shares would need a draw-ID dump (not implemented).

## Box-light measurement (jobs `boxlights-S5/S6/S7`, 2026-10-27)

Per scene, one session with two traces: `ls_debug=4` (the D-LS validation view: lighting
= |game − recompute| × 4 with the AO and sun zeroed; the recompute has no projector lights,
so on projector pixels the difference is the box lights' direct light), then `ls_debug=0`.

| Scene | Box lights listed/px | Point/spot listed/px | Box direct light / lit luminance | Pixels where box > 5% / > 20% of the light |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S5 Grymforge | 2.10-2.17 | 6.09 | about 8% | 81% / 8% |
| S6 Adamantine Forge | 2.61-2.62 | 4.28 | about 15% | 95% / 29% |
| S7 Blushing Mermaid | 2.76-2.77 | 7.09 | about 8% | 58% / 4% |

- **Method:** the traces' compose dump minus the effects target, the val trace's difference
  divided by 4 against the base trace's lit luminance, from the Reinhard previews inverted.
- **These are upper bounds.** Compose also carries fog in-scatter, which the val view does
  not zero. S7's validation is off even without projectors (game = 0.87 × recompute + 0.0095
  on the lit pixels), and S5's val trace is from an earlier session than its base.
- **What the box lights light:**
  - S7: the characters, through their Rim/Key/Fill rigs, the image the art direction cares
    about most;
  - S6: the volumetric light shafts and their floor pools;
  - S5: a broad low fill.
- **Conclusion:** gap 2 stays second. The box lights are a third of the listed lights and
  up to 15% of the light, concentrated on characters and shafts, all with game shadows
  only.
