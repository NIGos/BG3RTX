Version 0.9.7-beta

Lighting
- Sun shadows under roofs, far bounce light and per-light data work again (Medium, High and Ultra): since game Patch 8 BG3RTX no longer recognised the level. It now recognises it from the game's lights, also right after a load (about 0.2 ms).

Fixes
- Play on a fresh install saves the shown preset again (High and Ultra had run without opacity micromaps and cutout masks).
- The launcher asks for a restart when Hair shadows or Detailed logging are turned on while playing.
- 17 of the 71 cutout masks, rejected at every start, now load (about 36 MB more video memory).

Known
- Tested only on an RTX 5090 with driver 621.01.
- Everything else as in 0.9.6-beta (see its changelog).

Source code: https://github.com/NIGos/BG3RTX (GPL-3.0-or-later with the NVIDIA SDK exception).
